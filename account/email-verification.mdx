---
title: "Verify your email"
description: "Confirm the email address on your Byg account."
icon: "envelope"
---

1. Open [Email verification](https://byg.gg/email-verification), or use the verification banner shown after signing in.
2. Enter the six-digit code sent to your account email.
3. Select **Verify Email**.

If the message did not arrive, select **Resend Code** and check your spam or junk folder. You can also verify from [Settings → Security](https://byg.gg/settings#security).
