---
title: "Sign up"
description: "Create a Byg account."
icon: "user-plus"
---

1. Open [Byg sign up](https://byg.gg/signup).
2. Enter your email address, username, and password.
3. Select **Sign Up**.

You are signed in after the account is created. Byg may ask you to verify the email address before you use all account features. See [Verify your email](/account/email-verification).
