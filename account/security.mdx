---
title: "Account security"
description: "Keep your Byg account protected."
icon: "shield"
---

Use a unique password that you do not reuse elsewhere. Do not share your password, verification codes, authenticator codes, or session tokens.

Recommended account protections:

1. Keep your email verified in [Security settings](https://byg.gg/settings#security).
2. Enable [two-factor authentication](/account/two-factor).
3. Review [active sessions](/account/sessions) and revoke anything you do not recognize.
4. Sign out when using a shared or public device.

Byg currently provides email verification, authenticator-app 2FA, and session controls. There is no password-change or password-reset screen in the current Web client; contact [Byg Support](mailto:hi@byg.gg) if you lose access.
