---
title: "Manage sessions"
description: "Review where your Byg account is signed in."
icon: "laptop-mobile"
---

Open [Sessions settings](https://byg.gg/settings#sessions). Each session can show its device, approximate country, IP address, last-used time, and expiry state.

- Select **Remove** to revoke a session.
- Select **Enable Expiry** to make a session expire.
- Select **Disable Expiry** to keep a session from expiring automatically.

Revoke any session you do not recognize. Removing the current session signs you out of that device.
