---
title: "Change the theme"
description: "Customize the Byg interface appearance."
icon: "palette"
---

Open [Theme settings](https://byg.gg/settings#interface).

Under **Themes**, choose one of the two modes:

- **Clear** — use a wallpaper URL. Paste a direct public image URL into **Wallpaper URL**.
- **Color** — choose one of Byg's built-in color themes.

Changes are saved as your interface preference. A wallpaper URL must serve an image directly; see [Add images](/web/images) for help finding one.
