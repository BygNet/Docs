---
title: "Edit your profile"
description: "Update the public information on your Byg profile."
icon: "user"
---

Open [Profile settings](https://byg.gg/settings#profile), edit the fields you need, and select **Save Changes**.

Available fields include:

- Display name and pronouns
- Bio
- Avatar URL and banner URL
- A `song.link` URL for your profile music card
- Profile accent color, for eligible paid accounts

Avatar and banner fields need direct public image URLs. See [Add images](/web/images) for how to find one.
