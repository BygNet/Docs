## Start

1. [Create an account](https://byg.gg/signup), or [log in](https://byg.gg/login).
2. Open [Byg Social](https://byg.gg/).
3. Use the navigation to open Social, Gallery, Chat, Profile, Search, and Settings.

On desktop, navigation is in the left sidebar. On mobile, it is in the bottom tab bar.

## Useful links

- [Explore](https://byg.gg/explore) — dashboard and Byg app launcher.
- [Write a post](https://byg.gg/#create) — open the post creator.
- [Gallery](https://byg.gg/gallery) — browse images.
- [Profile settings](https://byg.gg/settings#profile) — edit your profile.
- [Interface settings](https://byg.gg/settings#interface) — change language and themes.
